## 1. Wish / If only

*If only* — то же, что *I wish*, только эмоциональнее.

| Сожалеем о… | Конструкция | Пример |
|---|---|---|
| **настоящем** (сейчас не так) | wish + **Past Simple** (*were* для всех лиц) | *I **wish** I **were** braver.* / *If only we **didn't need** weapons.* |
| **прошлом** (тогда было не так) | wish + **Past Perfect** | *She **wishes** she **had stayed** in her country.* |
| **умении** | wish + **could** | *If only I **could speak** better about human rights.* |
| **чужом поведении** (хотим, чтобы **другие** изменились; часто раздражение) | wish + **would** | *I **wish** people **would wake up** to injustice.* |

:::warn Две ловушки
1. **Нельзя** *I wish I would…* о себе: *wish + would* — только про других или про ситуацию (✗ *I wish I would have more time* → ✓ *I wish I **had** more time*).
2. С глаголами состояния (*have, be, know, need*) — Past Simple, а не *would*: ✓ *They wish they **had** the power…*
:::

## 2. Would rather / would prefer

| Кто действует | Конструкция | Пример |
|---|---|---|
| **тот же человек** | would rather + **инфинитив без to** | *I'd rather **not think** about it.* / *She'd rather **live** at home.* |
| **другой человек** | would rather + subject + **Past Simple** | *I'd rather you **drove**.* / *I'd rather you **didn't speak** to them.* |
| would prefer (другой) | would prefer + subject + **Past Simple** (или *for sb to do*) | *Would you prefer we **donated** money?* |

## 3. Mixed conditionals

| Тип | Условие → результат | Схема | Пример |
|---|---|---|---|
| прошлое → настоящее | что-то случилось/не случилось **тогда** — последствия **сейчас** | If + Past Perfect, would + V | *If the war **had ended** earlier, the city **would be** rich now.* |
| настоящее → прошлое | постоянная черта **сейчас** объясняет **прошлое** | If + Past Simple, would have + V3 | *If I **were** more confident, I **would have spoken** at the debate.* |

## 4. Ответы к Task 3 (*choose the correct option*)

1. I wish governments **would spend** more money on education… — *желание, чтобы **другие** изменили поведение* (вариант *spent* тоже грамматически возможен, но здесь ожидается *would*).
2. If only Aung San Suu Kyi **hadn't spent** 15 years in prison… — *сожаление о прошлом* → Past Perfect.
3. Would you rather **volunteer** your time, or would you prefer we **donated** money? — *rather* + инфинитив (тот же человек); *prefer we* + Past Simple (другие люди).
4. Most world leaders wish they **had** the power… — *have* как состояние → Past Simple.
5. If only we **didn't need** weapons at all… — *need* — состояние, настоящее → Past Simple.
6. If only I **could speak** better about human rights. — *умение* → could.
7. Shirin Ebadi wishes that she **had stayed** in her country… She'd much rather **live** at home than in exile. — прошлое → Past Perfect; *rather* + инфинитив.
8. I wish people **would wake up** to the problems… — *другие люди* → would.

## 5. Ответы к Task 4 (*complete the conversations*)

1. I'd rather you **drove**.
2. If only I **had** enough money…
3. I wish people **would stop** complaining…
4. I wish I **had bought** one with a smaller engine.
5. I'd rather **not think** about it.
6. I wish each country **would become** more independent…
7. No, but I wish I **had seen** it.
8. I'd rather you **didn't speak** to them.

:::task Проверь себя
1. If only I ___ (know) the answer yesterday!
2. I wish my brother ___ (stop) using my laptop without asking.
3. I'd rather we ___ (meet) tomorrow, not today.
4. If I ___ (not / miss) the bus this morning, I wouldn't be late now.
:::answer
1. had known — 2. would stop — 3. met — 4. hadn't missed (mixed conditional: прошлое → настоящее).
:::
